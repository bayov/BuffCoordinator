Adventor BC is TeX Gyre Adventor 2.501 (https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/adventor),
a free font in the style of ITC Avant Garde Gothic, by B. Jackowski, J.M. Nowacki and the
GUST e-foundry. It's distributed under the GUST Font License ("Adventor License.txt").

Buff Coordinator ships it converted from OpenType (CFF) to TrueType outlines, since the game
loads TrueType files. As the license asks of derived works, the family is renamed from
"TeX Gyre Adventor" to "Adventor BC" and the files are renamed:

  texgyreadventor-regular.otf  ->  Adventor-Regular.ttf
  texgyreadventor-bold.otf     ->  Adventor-Bold.ttf

Nothing else about the glyphs or metrics changed.
